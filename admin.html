How to Customize Subfolders in Google Drive:
1. Logo: Drop an image named logo.png or icon.png inside the subfolder. It will appear centered on the folder front!
2. Color: Add a file named color.txt containing a hex color (e.g. #0a84ff or #ff453a) to customize its gradient.

Cache & Re-Sync

Whenever you add, delete, or rename files or subfolders in Google Drive, flush the cache to force Google Drive to re-index all assets.
